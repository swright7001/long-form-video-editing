# Clip style references

These two styles were approved by the original creator. For other channels they are customizable starters, not inherited user approvals. Follow the current creator’s profile and brief. If no style is specified, choose by tone, state the assumption and proceed. Keep a consistent style within a batch unless variation is requested.

## Shared composition

- Edge-to-edge video, typically 9:16 for portrait delivery. No decorative top/bottom borders, headline panels, footer cards, discussion prompts or surrounding phone frame.
- Live captions directly over the footage, synchronized to actual speech. Use short readable phrase groups and timed emphasis; verify timing against audio. If word timing is unavailable, do not fabricate it.
- Place captions around the lower chest region, approximately 68–78% down the frame as a starting point, adjusting for the subject, microphone, and platform controls. Keep text clear of faces and leave comfortable side margins.
- Keep a small readable source credit visible. Replace SimpMe with the actual source identity for other footage; preserve the source URL in project notes and requested post copy. Do not invent a handle or imply ownership of someone else's footage.
- These images are visual layout references generated by AI. Use original video frames in actual edits, not the generated faces, reconstructed studio, or image text. Do not recreate or alter the speaker with AI as part of applying the template. Crop or compose real footage without stretching or losing essential context.
- The approved images demonstrate style, not a transcript or fixed caption wording. All captions must come from the actual clip.

## Yellow highlight

Visual reference: [Approved yellow-highlight example](../assets/approved-yellow-highlight.png).

Bold, condensed uppercase white captions, centered in one or two balanced lines. Emphasize the current spoken word or short phrase with black lettering on a compact yellow rectangular highlight. Keep remaining words white with subtle dark shadow or stroke for contrast. The highlight closely fits the emphasized words; it does not become a full-width panel.

Small white source name at the upper right with a smaller `SOURCE` label beneath, inset from the edges. No badge background by default. Prefer this treatment for emphatic hooks or energetic delivery.

## Cyan underline

Visual reference: [Approved cyan-underline example](../assets/approved-cyan-underline.png).

Clean, medium-bold white sentence-case captions, left aligned, normally one or two lines, with subtle dark shadow or stroke. A thin cyan underline tracks the emphasized spoken word or short phrase. Preserve readable pacing; avoid distracting bouncing or rapid decorative effects.

Small white `Source: <name>` credit at the upper left, without a background badge. Prefer this treatment for conversational or reflective clips.

## Implementation and review

Match proportions and hierarchy from the saved reference; font and color values may be approximated using available licensed fonts and tuned against real footage. Verify at delivery resolution and phone scale, including bright backgrounds. The original approval covers composition, not a precise font license or universal pixel measurement. Current creator preferences take precedence. Preserve prior exports; rerender them only when requested.
